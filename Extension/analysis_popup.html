Authentication

SPF

Waiting for data...

What it means: Checks whether the server sending the email is authorized to send messages for the evaluated domain.

Why it matters: Helps identify emails sent from servers that may not be authorized by the domain owner.

DKIM

Waiting for data...

What it means: Uses a digital signature to verify that signed portions of the email have not been altered.

Why it matters: Helps verify the integrity of the signed email content and its association with a signing domain.

DMARC

Waiting for data...

What it means: Checks whether SPF or DKIM authentication aligns with the domain shown in the sender's address.

Why it matters: Helps domain owners protect their domains against unauthorized use and email spoofing.
